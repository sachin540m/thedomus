Welcome to The Domus Élanza, where expansive residences with horizontal living rooms open to generous decks, framing serene lake and lush green views, amid open landscapes designed for a perfectly balanced life.

The art of living, perfected

An exclusive development offering spacious three-bedroom and larger residences with vast horizontal living rooms, awash with air, light and space. These flow seamlessly onto expansive decks, creating homes that most city living has long forgotten.

Picture Perfect

Breath-taking views unfold on both sides — expansive vistas of Yamare Lake and surrounding greens on the outside, and lush central greens and amenities within, framing every residence in openness and serenity.

Perfect life deserves room to breathe

Beyond your doorstep, the landscape unfurls like a living tapestry of cascading greens, terraced promenades and thematic gardens. Expansive open spaces replace urban clutter, shaping an environment anchored in openness, wellness and quiet grace.

Timeless elegance, with modern grace

The towers rise with a modern grace, yet carry the unmistakable imprint of Art Deco — a design language synonymous with timeless elegance. Curved balconies, patterned screens and bespoke railings give the development its iconic silhouette.

Sophisticated. Effortless. Unforgettable.

At The Domus Élanza, leisure is not an afterthought - it’s an art form. The grand clubhouse anchors a thoughtfully curated world of amenities, from fitness and indoor games to spaces for celebration and calm reflection. Outdoors, landscaped leisure zones, sports areas and tranquil corners extend everyday living beyond the home. Every space is designed for both indulgence and ease, ensuring life unfolds with quiet grace.

Most Common Queries

What is The Domus Élanza Sarjapur Road?

The Domus Élanza Sarjapur Road is a premium residential development offering luxury apartments in Bangalore. Spread across a thoughtfully planned master layout with expansive open spaces, it features spacious 3 BHK apartments designed for modern urban living. With excellent connectivity and a serene environment, The Domus Élanza stands out among new apartments for sale in Bangalore and is an ideal choice for homebuyers exploring flats in Sarjapur Road.

Where is The Domus Élanza located?

The Domus Élanza is located off Sarjapur Road near Sompura, East Bangalore, one of the most sought-after residential corridors for homebuyers looking for flats in Bangalore. The project offers a rare combination of urban connectivity and serene surroundings, making it an ideal choice for those seeking luxury apartments in Bangalore.

How is the connectivity from The Domus Élanza Sarjapur Road to other parts of Bangalore?

The Domus Élanza enjoys excellent connectivity to major parts of the city via Sarjapur Road, with quick access to Outer Ring Road (ORR) and the upcoming Peripheral Ring Road (PRR). This ensures seamless travel to IT hubs such as Whitefield, Electronic City, HSR Layout, Koramangala, and Marathahalli, making it a preferred option among apartments for sale in Bangalore.

What key lifestyle, retail, and commercial destinations are near The Domus Élanza Sarjapur Road?

Among the most well-connected apartments in Sarjapur Road, The Domus Élanza is located within a 30-minute drive of essential urban conveniences –
IT & Commercial Hubs: RGA Tech Park, Embassy Tech Village, Prestige Tech Park, Cessna Business Park, Wipro, Microsoft
Educational Institutions: Oakridge International School, TISB, Greenwood High, Inventure Academy, Azim Premji University
Healthcare Facilities: Swastik Hospital, Motherhood Hospital, Manipal Hospital
Retail & Leisure: DMart, Decathlon, Myhna Square Mall, Market Square Mall
This strategic location makes The Domus Élanza a strong choice for those exploring Sarjapur flats for sale.

What apartment configurations are available at The Domus Élanza?

The Domus Élanza Sarjapur Road offers premium 3 BHK and 3 BHK with study apartments, designed for modern urban living. These spacious homes are ideal for families planning to buy a flat in Bangalore, especially those seeking 3 BHK flats in Bangalore within a well-planned residential community.

What amenities are available at The Domus Élanza Sarjapur Road?

Positioned among the finest luxury apartments in Bangalore, The Domus Élanza Sarjapur Road offers an extensive range of lifestyle amenities, including a landscaped central park, walking and jogging track, luxury clubhouse with gym, café, and indoor games, temperature-controlled swimming pool, outdoor sports courts, children’s play zone, yoga and meditation garden, amphitheatre, star-gazing platform, and temple within the premises. These amenities elevate The Domus Élanza as one of the most desirable new apartments for sale in Bangalore.

What views can residents enjoy from the decks at The Domus Élanza Sarjapur Road?

The private decks at The Domus Élanza Sarjapur Road offer tranquil lake views and lush green landscapes, creating a calming retreat from city life. These scenic vistas add to the appeal of these new flats in Bangalore, making everyday living visually refreshing and peaceful.

Who should consider buying a home at The Domus Élanza Sarjapur Road?
