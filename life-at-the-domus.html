Life at The Domus

Crafting spaces designed to be lived. Residential, hospitality and material expertise brought together with intention.

Three disciplines. One commitment.

We combine architectural design, technical construction, and precision delivery under a single cohesive studio workflow to craft timeless residential projects.

Our Core Services

Residential, hospitality and material expertise brought together with intention.

Residential Interiors

Full interior architecture for private homes — from spatial planning through material selection, bespoke joinery design, and final styling.

• Spatial planning & layout optimisation
• Material palette sourcing & styling
• Bespoke furniture & joinery design
• Lighting design & specification

Hospitality Design

Interiors for boutique hotels and restaurants, built around guest flow, material longevity, and sensory sequencing.

• Boutique hotels (8–40 keys)
• Restaurant & bar interiors
• Guest-flow sequencing & wayfinding
• Durability & atmosphere specification

Material Consultancy

Independent material selection and sourcing curating natural stone, specialist plaster, timber, and hardware longevity.

• Specification review & provenance
• Supplier network access & introductions
• On-site material review sessions
• Written specifications & schedules

Our Design Process

Four stages. No shortcuts. Quality requires attention, not speed.

STAGE 01: Site & Light Study

We spend a full day on site across morning and evening light before any drawing begins.

STAGE 02: Spatial Concept

A single organising idea is developed and tested against every room in the brief.

STAGE 03: Material Palette

Physical swatches assembled and reviewed on site, never under studio lighting alone.

STAGE 04: Execution & Craft

Our site team works alongside named artisans through to final handover.
