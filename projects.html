The Domus Prive

Situated in Walkeshwar, opposite the Governor’s Estate, The Domus Prive stands at one of Mumbai’s most distinguished addresses.
Here, an Art Deco landmark rises on the skyline, rare in its time and place.

Bespoke craftsmanship at an elite scale

Set on one of the largest land parcels in Malabar Hill, every residence commands sweeping sea and green views, uninterrupted and enduring.

A study in raw materiality

The Domus Prive is envisioned as a contemporary tribute to Art Deco, shaped by intricate detailing and enduring craftsmanship. A design language that once defined a city now finds new expression here.

Spaces configured for modern living

A private members’ club for a select few, thoughtfully bringing together hospitality, wellness and leisure. Curated with discreet precision, every space is designed for quiet ease & privacy.

Quiet, anticipatory design

Here, service is anticipatory and deeply personal. Delivered by The Domus’s in-house hospitality team, it brings the attentiveness of a five-star hotel into the comfort and privacy of your own home.

Creators of the World's Finest Developments

Recognising the profound impact of design and service on well-being, we create thoughtful and purposeful living spaces.

Committed to surpassing expectations, our carefully crafted events and experiences ensure an enriched and empowered lifestyle.

We are creating homes and workspaces for an ever-changing tomorrow, where luxury and sustainability can coexist in perfect harmony.
